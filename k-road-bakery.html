About us · Est. 2018

Baked fresh in Auckland since 2018.

We started with one sourdough starter, a borrowed mixer and a belief that a good bakery should feel like part of the neighbourhood.

Today, our tiny team still makes every loaf by hand, works with local growers and roasters, and keeps the coffee strong. Come in for a loaf, stay for a little while.
